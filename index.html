04 • Debate Room

Take a position.

Good political thinking means being able to defend an argument and understand the strongest counterargument.

PROPOSITION

“A democracy should sometimes restrict individual freedom to protect the democratic order.”

  • What would a security argument say?
  • What would a civil-liberties argument say?
  • Where should the limit be?

YOUR CHALLENGE

Write a 3-part argument:

  • Claim — what do you believe?
  • Reason — why?
  • Counterargument — what is the strongest objection?
